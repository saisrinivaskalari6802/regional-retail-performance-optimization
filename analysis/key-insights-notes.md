# Analysis Notes & Key Insights

## Regional Performance
- Three regions consistently under-indexed on both AOV and repeat rate.
- Urban flagship stores outperformed suburban formats on conversion but showed higher CAC on paid social.

## Campaign Effectiveness
- Email maintained stable ROAS (typically 2.1–2.4x) across regions.
- Paid social ROAS varied significantly by store format; urban performed well, suburban lagged.
- Loyalty-sourced customers showed higher downstream retention, supporting continued investment in that acquisition path.

## Customer Behavior
- 90-day retention was materially higher for loyalty-tier acquisitions versus pure digital cold traffic.
- Repeat revenue share was a stronger leading indicator of regional health than traffic alone.

## Inventory Alignment
- High contribution-margin SKUs were under-stocked relative to their revenue contribution in the lagging regions.
- Weeks-of-supply flags highlighted excess inventory in lower-margin categories.

## Data Quality Observations
- Initial discrepancy rate vs finance extracts was elevated; after standardization of region codes and guest vs loyalty handling, reconciliation improved significantly.
- Missing customer_id on a portion of transactions required careful treatment in retention metrics (guest exclusion or probabilistic matching notes documented).

These notes informed the final recommendations delivered to merchandising and marketing leadership.
