# Dashboard insights — how to read each view

These notes sit next to the live dashboard pages. They are the comments an analyst would give in a weekly commercial stand-up.

## Executive

**Monthly trend.** Northeast and West still compound month to month. Southeast is flat from January through June. If the gap were “a bad month,” the line would dip and recover. It does not.

**Revenue by region.** Rank order (Northeast first) is not the decision. Gap to target is. Southeast −8.7%, Midwest −5.1%.

**Vs target table.** Status is AOV-driven. Coastal AOV is $92–$94. Lagging regions are $78–$82. Traffic volume is not the shortfall.

**Category mix vs margin.** Apparel is 54% of sales (volume). Accessories are 18% of sales and 56% margin (profit). Lagging regions under-index accessories and high-margin outerwear.

**What to say:** Do not buy more traffic. Fix AOV (mix + offer) and repeat (loyalty).

## Campaign

**ROAS bars.** Email 2.42x, Loyalty 2.18x, Search 1.95x, Paid social 1.62x, Display 1.15x. Paid social has the largest budget.

**Spend table.** Attribution uses a 7-day click window. Display looks worse if you apply contribution margin; treat it as the first cut.

**Action:** Reallocate 12–15% of suburban paid social to Email and Loyalty. Keep urban flagship social.

## Retention

**90-day rate.** Loyalty 41.2% vs paid social 28.4% (~27% relative lift). Guest transactions are excluded so unidentified checkouts do not inflate the denominator.

**Action:** Attach a loyalty offer to paid social in suburban formats. Protect email spend; it is both efficient and durable.

## Inventory

**Weeks of supply.** High-margin SKUs in Southeast/Midwest sit under 3 weeks (stockout risk). Low-margin basics sit over 14 weeks (excess cash).

**Action:** Open-to-buy and transfers toward outerwear, sneakers, bags in lagging regions. Do not replenish excess basics.

## Definitions (so comments stay honest)

| Metric | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| Revenue | Net of discount, returns excluded from this mart |
| AOV | Net revenue / transactions |
| ROAS | Attributed revenue / media spend, 7-day click |
| 90-day retention | Repeat purchase within 90 days of first identified purchase |
| Weeks of supply | On-hand units / average weekly units sold |
