Most retail teams still wait 7–10 days for reporting that should land overnight.

I just wrapped an end-to-end analytics case study that treats that lag as the actual problem — not another dashboard for its own sake.

Project: Regional Retail Performance & Campaign Effectiveness Optimization
A national apparel chain. 180+ stores plus e-commerce. Siloed POS, CRM, and campaign data.

What I built
→ Integrated 1.2M+ transactions into a governed star schema (SQL: CTEs, window functions, quality gates)
→ Executive reporting on revenue, AOV, ROAS, and 90-day retention
→ Campaign attribution with a 7-day window and cohort analysis by acquisition channel
→ Recommendations to reallocate 12–15% of paid social toward higher-ROAS email and loyalty in lagging regions

What the numbers showed
• ~40% reduction in manual reporting effort
• Cycle time cut from 7–10 days to next business day
• Southeast & Midwest shortfall was AOV and repeat rate — not traffic
• Loyalty-acquired customers retained 27% better at 90 days

Stack: SQL · Power BI · DAX · Excel / Power Query

Case study and dashboard walkthrough are on my portfolio. GitHub has the SQL, measure catalog, and documentation.
