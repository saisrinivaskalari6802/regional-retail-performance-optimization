# Final Dashboard Page Specifications

## Page 1 – Executive Summary
**Audience:** VP Merchandising, Director of Marketing, Finance

**Visuals:**
- 4 KPI cards: Total Revenue, AOV, Campaign ROAS, 90-Day Retention (with sparklines / vs prior period)
- Regional performance table (Revenue, AOV, vs Target, Status)
- Key Alerts callout box
- Optional: small regional heatmap or contribution waterfall

**Filters:** Date range (default last 12 months), Channel

## Page 2 – Regional Performance
**Visuals:**
- Horizontal bar: Revenue by Region
- Horizontal bar: AOV by Region
- Matrix or table: Region × Channel with Revenue, Units, Margin %
- Insight text box or smart narrative

**Interactions:** Click region → filters other visuals; drill-through to store level

## Page 3 – Campaign Effectiveness
**Visuals:**
- ROAS by Channel (sorted bars)
- Spend vs Attributed Revenue table or clustered bar
- Trend line: Weekly ROAS by top channels
- Recommendation / action callout

**Filters:** Campaign type, Date, Region

## Page 4 – Customer Retention
**Visuals:**
- Column chart: 90-Day Retention by Acquisition Channel
- Cohort matrix (optional, if pre-aggregated)
- Three insight cards (Loyalty advantage, Email quality, Social reality)

## Page 5 – Inventory Alignment (optional extension)
**Visuals:**
- Sell-through % vs Weeks of Supply scatter or dual-axis
- Top / bottom SKUs by contribution margin in lagging regions
- Action flags for over/under stocked high-margin items

## Cross-Page Standards
- Consistent color: Teal = positive / on-track, Amber = watch, Red = at risk
- Tooltip includes measure definition + last refresh
- Bookmarks: “Underperforming Regions”, “High-ROAS Channels”, “Loyalty Focus”
- Footer with refresh timestamp and data as-of date
