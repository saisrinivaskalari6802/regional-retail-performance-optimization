# Recommendations delivered

1. **Budget reallocation** — Move 12–15% of paid social spend into Email and Loyalty in Southeast and Midwest. Paid social remains useful in urban/flagship formats.
2. **Assortment** — Increase open-to-buy on high contribution-margin SKUs in lagging regions; reduce excess weeks of supply in low-margin categories.
3. **Targeting** — Use simple RFM / loyalty-tier rules for the next two campaign waves. Loyalty-acquired customers retained ~27% better at 90 days.
4. **Operating rhythm** — Replace ad-hoc Excel packs with a weekly stand-up using the dashboard as the single source of truth.

These are directional pilot recommendations, not a guarantee of future lift.
