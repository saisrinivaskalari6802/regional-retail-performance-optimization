# Business Requirements – Regional Retail Performance & Campaign Effectiveness

## Stakeholders
- VP of Merchandising (executive sponsor)
- Director of Digital Marketing
- Regional Merchandising Managers
- Finance FP&A (revenue reconciliation)
- Commercial Analytics team

## Problem Statement
Revenue growth decelerated to low single digits while marketing spend increased. Reporting was siloed (store POS vs digital vs campaign platforms), manual, and lagged 7–10 days. Decision makers could not quickly identify which regions, channels, or campaigns were underperforming or where inventory investment was misaligned with contribution margin.

## Success Criteria
- Reporting latency ≤ 1 business day
- Manual report production time reduced ≥ 35%
- Data discrepancy rate (vs finance GL) < 5%
- Self-service adoption by at least the core merchandising and marketing users within 30 days of go-live
- Clear attribution of campaign contribution and regional performance drivers

## Priority Use Cases
1. Weekly regional performance stand-up (revenue, AOV, units, margin)
2. Campaign effectiveness review (spend, ROAS, channel mix, attributed revenue)
3. Customer retention and acquisition channel quality
4. Inventory alignment (sell-through vs weeks of supply for high-margin SKUs)
5. Ad-hoc root-cause questions on underperforming stores or categories

## Out of Scope (Pilot)
- Real-time streaming
- Predictive demand forecasting models
- Full customer lifetime value scoring engine (basic RFM and retention cohorts only)
- Integration with supply-chain planning systems beyond inventory snapshots
